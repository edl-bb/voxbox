VoxBox — logo concepts (Cubbei Studios)

Four directions, each built as flat layered vector (background + glyph) so they drop straight into Apple's Icon Composer for the macOS 26 Liquid Glass icon treatment. Shown at dock size and small size.

1 · Wavebox

The safe, instantly-readable one. Purple gradient, bold voice bars. Reads perfectly at 16px.

2 · Voice bubble

Speech bubble carrying a waveform — "spoken words become text". Friendly, communicative.

3 · V-wave monogram

The V of VoxBox drawn as a plunging waveform on a premium dark tile. Strongest brand mark; pairs well with a wordmark.

4 · Out of the box

Sound rising out of an open box — the literal VoxBox. Playful, ownable, most distinctive silhouette.
